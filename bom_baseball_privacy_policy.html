Privacy Policy for Book of Mormon Baseball

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Book of Mormon Baseball ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing an enjoyable gaming and educational experience.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require an account and collects no name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location data. It works as follows:

  • Game data: Your statistics, achievements, settings and scores are stored only on your device. The game's questions are bundled with the app and stored in a local database on your device. None of this is sent to us.
  • Advertising data: The app shows banner advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information, advertising identifiers, IP address (including approximate location derived from it) and ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads. This is handled according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Support messages: If you choose "Report a problem" in the app, your email app opens with a pre-filled message. Only what you choose to send is received by us.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Provide core game functionality
  • Save your game progress and preferences locally
  • Display relevant advertisements through AdMob
  • Improve game performance and user experience

4. Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob for advertising. AdMob may collect and use data according to their privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Information

Consent choices. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can review or change your choice at any time under Settings, "Ad privacy choices". On iOS the app asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before accessing the advertising identifier; if you decline, ads are not personalized using that identifier.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings (Ad Settings on Android, or Limit Ad Tracking on iOS).

5. Data Storage

All game data (preferences, statistics, and game state) is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and a local database. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers. If you uninstall the app, this local data will be deleted.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed as a general audience educational trivia game and is not directed to children under 13. Ads are configured for a general audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data. Since game data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for securing your device with appropriate passwords or biometric authentication.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your locally stored game data through the app interface
  • Delete your data by clearing app data in device settings or uninstalling the app
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through device settings or the in-app "Ad privacy choices"
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, delete or restrict the processing of personal data held about you, and to withdraw consent. We do not hold personal data about you ourselves; the advertising data described above is held by Google, and you can exercise your rights with Google at the links in section 4.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. International Users

This app is available worldwide. By using the app, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this policy, regardless of your location.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at pendergamification@gmail.com, or use "Report a problem" in the app's Settings.

12. Consent

By using Book of Mormon Baseball, you consent to this privacy policy and agree to its terms.
