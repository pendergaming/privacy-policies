Arcade-opolis Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: Arcade-opolis has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. Your game progress stays on your device. Data about you is collected only by Google AdMob, which shows the banner and optional rewarded ads that keep the app free.

Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the Arcade-opolis mobile application (the "App") created by Pender Gaming ("we", "us", "our"). The App is a free collection of six arcade-style games – Fish Food Frenzy, Soaring Puffin, Road Warrior, Glowwyrm, Tidelight and Moth & Lantern – guided by a mascot called Pixel. It supports Android and iOS.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any account or sign-in details (the App has no accounts)
  • Your precise location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone data (the App does not request these permissions)
  • Payment or financial information (the App has no in-app purchases)

Information Stored on Your Device

To let you continue where you left off, the App stores the following only on your device, using the device's local storage:

  • Game progress: how many times you have played each game and the stars you have earned; scores, best runs, levels reached, unlocked characters or items, settings such as sound, music and vibration, and games left in progress.
  • Virtual currency and items: some games let you earn free virtual coins, fish, gems or items. They have no cash value, cannot be bought with money and cannot be exchanged for money or prizes.
  • Your choices: whether Pixel's tips and helper chip are shown.

This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the App removes it.

Reminder Notifications (Moth & Lantern)

Moth & Lantern can show reminders (for example, when your daily rewards are ready). These are scheduled locally on your device; no information is sent to us or to any server. On Android 13 and later and on iOS the App asks for permission first, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings or in the game's own settings. The game may also ask, at most occasionally, whether you would like to rate the App using the in-app review prompt provided by Google Play or Apple; your answer is handled by Google or Apple, not by us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob: banner ads and optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch, for example to receive a revive, a hint or an extra reward. There are no ads between games forced on you.

AdMob may collect and use certain information to serve and measure ads, including your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction data such as ad impressions and clicks, and crash and diagnostic information. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and on iOS it asks for App Tracking Transparency permission. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy options (shown where required), and you can reset or opt out of personalised ads through your device's advertising settings.

Permissions

The App uses the Internet and network-state permissions so that ads can load. On Android 13+ it can ask for the notification permission for Moth & Lantern reminders (see above). It does not request any other permissions.

Children

Arcade-opolis is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served by AdMob in line with Google's policies.

Data Security and Retention

We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to retain, sell or share. Data processed by AdMob is encrypted in transit and is subject to Google's retention practices.

Changes to This Policy
