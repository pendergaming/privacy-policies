Moth & Lantern Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Moth & Lantern ("the app") is a merge puzzle game published by Pender Gaming ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

We do not run accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address, location or any other personal information ourselves. Your game progress (your board, rooms restored, coins, gems, settings and which story cards you have seen) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or choosing "Start over" in Settings removes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. A small banner appears at the bottom of the screen, and you may choose to watch a rewarded video in exchange for a bonus (extra moonlight). There are no ads between actions, and no ad plays unless you tap the button to watch one.

AdMob may collect and use certain information to serve and measure ads, including your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction data such as ad impressions and clicks, and crash and diagnostic information. This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices, when that option is shown. You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads through your device's advertising settings.

Purchases

The app has an in-game shop that uses only in-game coins and gems earned by playing. It does not currently sell anything for real money and does not process payments.

Children

Moth & Lantern is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Data security and retention

We hold no user data, so there is nothing for us to retain, sell or share. Data processed by AdMob is encrypted in transit and is subject to Google's retention practices.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
