Privacy Policy for Word Weaver

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

In short: Word Weaver has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours. Your game progress stays on your device. The only data collected about you comes from Google AdMob, which shows the banner ad and the optional rewarded video ad (watched only if you ask for a hint). Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent before any ads are shown.

Introduction

This privacy policy applies to the Word Weaver mobile application (the "App") created by Pender Gaming ("we", "us", "our"). The App is a free word puzzle game, supported by advertising, and is provided "AS IS". This policy explains what information the App uses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect, store, or transmit any of the following ourselves:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Photos, files, contacts, microphone, or camera data (the App does not request these permissions)
  • Payment or financial information (the App has no in-app purchases)
  • Analytics or usage tracking data of our own

Data Stored on Your Device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following information locally on your device (using the device's standard app storage):

  • Game progress: the level you have reached in each difficulty, the words you have found in your current level, and the recent words used in your last few levels (so levels do not repeat them).
  • Hints and bonus words: your number of available free hints, the date your daily free hint was last used, and your count of bonus words found.
  • Daily puzzle: the date of your last completed daily puzzle and your streak.
  • Settings: your sound and vibration preferences, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free because it shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party service. The App shows:

  • A banner ad at the bottom of the main menu and game screens.
  • An optional rewarded video ad, shown only if you choose to watch one to receive a hint. The App never shows full-screen ads between levels.

To show, measure, and protect the integrity of ads, Google AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android)
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and operating system information (such as model, OS version, language, and screen size)
  • App information and interactions with ads (such as ad impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostic and performance information

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google uses data when you use partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The App uses the Internet and network-state permissions only so that ads can load, and the vibration permission for haptic feedback. Ads may require the advertising ID permission (AD_ID) on Android 13 and later.

Consent and Your Ad Choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and similar regions): where the law requires it, the App shows a consent message (Google's User Messaging Platform) when you first open it, before requesting any ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy choices inside the App (this option appears where it is required).
  • Personalized ads: you can limit or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads), where you can opt out of Ads Personalization and delete or reset your advertising ID. On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Google ad settings: manage Google's ads controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive your personal information from Google; we only receive aggregate information about ad performance and revenue.

Children's Privacy

Word Weaver is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your country, which is up to 16 in some regions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action. Parents can also use their device's parental controls and ad settings to limit advertising.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal information about you, there is no account or personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or state privacy laws in the United States), you may have rights regarding data processed by Google for advertising. You can exercise them through the consent and ad settings described above and through Google's privacy tools.

Your locally stored game data can be removed at any time by:

  • Using Settings → Reset progress inside the App, or
  • Clearing the App's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the App.

Data Retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset your progress, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies.

Security

Since the App does not transmit your game data anywhere, it is protected by your device's own security. Ad requests are sent to Google over the Internet using standard secure connections where available.

International Data Transfers

We do not transfer personal data. Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own, as described in Google's privacy policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
